# Post-hoc analyses — China food demand 2050 (Nature Food package)

Generated by `modules/post_analysis.py` from `results/results_long.csv`, `results/world/world_results_long.csv` and `results/footprints/*`.
All 2050 values; core scenarios BS / A1 (PTS) / C1 (MTS) / B1 (HDS).

## 1. Self-sufficiency and trade dependence (`ssr_paths.csv`, `ssr_2050_matrix.csv`, `import_dependence_soy_dairy.csv`)

- Soybean SSR stays critically low in every scenario: 2024 = 0.16; 2050 BS = 0.27, PTS = 0.27, MTS = 0.27, HDS = 0.27 — the healthy-diet transition raises soybean self-sufficiency only marginally because food-soy demand partially replaces feed-soy demand.
- Dairy SSR 2050: BS = 0.75, PTS = 0.63, MTS = 0.48, HDS = 0.37 — dairy is the one commodity whose import dependence *rises* under the healthy diet (mirrors the counter-cyclical world milk-powder price, WDM +20% under HDS).
- Staple grains (rice, wheat) move to full self-sufficiency and net-export pressure under MTS/HDS; maize import dependence falls sharply as feed demand contracts.

| Added sugar | 0-50 g/d | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 |

- Red meat: BS 143 and PTS 157 g/d are ~3-4x the EAT-Lancet upper bound (28 g/d); MTS 80 g/d roughly halves the excess; HDS 40 g/d reaches the healthy range.
- Fat energy share: PTS 42%E far above WHO 30%; MTS 37%E; HDS 33%E.
- Dairy remains below the CDG 300 g/d floor even under HDS (280 g/d) — the guideline gap that drives the counter-cyclical global dairy price signal.
