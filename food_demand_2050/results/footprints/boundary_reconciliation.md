# Boundary Reconciliation

Generated by `modules/footprints.py`.

## Accounts

- Account A, `direct_production_account.csv`: direct farm-gate carbon, crop blue water, crop fertiliser N and model physical harvested area. Livestock water, lifecycle land and supply-chain nitrogen are excluded because available coefficients include feed or upstream processes.
- Account B, `final_consumption_lca_account.csv`: final-product footprint sensitivity using `food_demand_total` for China and `FOO` for CASM-World. It is not added to Account A.

## Automated Checks

- PASS: No livestock coefficient with includes_feed=yes is summed with feed-crop production coefficients.
- PASS: No lifecycle land coefficient is used in the direct production account.
- PASS: No supply-chain nitrogen factor is added to direct crop fertilizer N.

## Scope Notes

- Global water and nitrogen in the direct account are crop-direct indicators only; they are no longer presented as complete global water or reactive-N totals.
- World coefficients are predominantly global-average commodity coefficients with China-specific coefficients where available; production changes are region-specific.
- `world_carbon_coverage.csv` reports commodity coverage for the model-covered farm-gate GHG subset.
