Primary-source checks for the revised framing, 2026-11-08

Kaplan and Schulhofer-Wohl (2017), Inflation at the Household Level
https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/working-papers/2017/wp2017-13
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304393217300879
Use: household inflation differs with purchased products and prices paid. The current
paper measures only the observed food sub-budget at provincial group-price proxies.

Chen et al. (2016), A Meta-analysis of Food Demand Elasticities for China
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1093/aepp/ppv006
Use: existing Chinese demand evidence varies across foods, income and specifications.

Hendel and Nevo (2006), Measuring the Implications of Sales and Consumer Inventory Behavior
https://www.nber.org/papers/w11307
Use: purchasing dynamics can distinguish short-run purchases from longer-run demand.
The present recording-window comparisons do not estimate an inventory model.

Lewbel and Pendakur (2009), Tricks with Hicks: The EASI Demand System
https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.99.3.827
Use: distinguish the approximate Stone implementation from implicit exact EASI.

Hovhannisyan and Bozic (2017), Price Endogeneity and Food Demand in Urban China
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1477-9552.12175
Use: external price measurement and causal price identification are different questions.

No literature source is used as evidence for this paper's numerical findings.
All numerical findings are traced to the supplied Stata outputs and the new cost check.
