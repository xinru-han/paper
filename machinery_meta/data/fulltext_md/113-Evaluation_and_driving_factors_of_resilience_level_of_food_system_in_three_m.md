# Evaluation and driving factors of resilience level of food system in three major functional zones of China

haiqi Zhang1

Received: 27 March 2024 / Accepted: 26 November 2024

## Abstract

Against the backdrop of deepening globalization, increasingly severe climate change, and increasing frequency of various “black swan" and “gray rhinoceros" events, the importance of resilience in the food system is becoming increasingly prominent. This article uses panel data from 31 provinces in China from 2013 to 2022 to construct a resilience index system for the food system. The entropy method and geographic detector are used to analyze the level, spatiotemporal evolution characteristics, and influencing factors of resilience in different food functional areas in China. The main conclusions are as follows: from 2013 to 2022, China's main food producing areas, main sales areas, and production sales balance areas all achieved an increase in resilience level. In terms of different sub dimensions, there are differences in the changes of different functional areas. In terms of spatiotemporal evolution characteristics, from 2013 to 2022, the resilience of the food system in different functional areas of China has increased, and the changes in different provinces are clearly visible. In terms of influencing factors, unit food yield, multiple cropping index, rural primary industry personnel, and agricultural mechanization level are the main influencing factors on the resilience level of the food system in different functional areas of China. The interaction between each influencing factor is mainly dual factor enhancement and non-linear enhancement.

Keywords Food system · Food security · Crop index · Geodetector · Entropy method

## 1 Introduction

The importance of food security lies in its importance as a crucial foundation for national security and social stability and is crucial for the survival and development of a country and its people. The importance of food security lies in its importance as a crucial foundation for national security, social stability, and the survival and development of a country and its people. As a country with a large population, ensuring food security is particularly important for China, as it directly relates to the long-term stability and sustainable development of the country. However, there are many issues that currently threaten China's food security. Firstly, there is a decrease in arable land resources. With the rapid development of urbanization and industrialization, the cultivated land area is constantly decreasing, which poses pressure on food production. The decline in arable land quality and the phenomenon of non-agriculturalization of arable land also pose challenges to food security (Fei et al., 2021). Secondly, the increase in extreme weather events caused by global climate change, such as droughts, floods, and heatwaves has had a negative impact on food production, reducing food production and quality (Gao & Liu, 2014). Thirdly, there is a shortage of water resources. The shortage and uneven distribution of water resources have posed restrictions on China's food production, especially in the northern regions where the shortage of water resources poses a serious threat to agricultural production (Weijing et al., 2023). Fourthly, there is pressure on the agricultural ecological environment. For a long time, in order to ensure food supply, China's agricultural production has been pursuing yield, abusing and overusing inputs such as fertilizers and pesticides, resulting in damage to the agricultural ecological environment and weakened sustainable development capacity of agriculture (Luo et al., 2023). Fifth, the fragility of the food supply chain (Zhang, 2024). The fragility of the global food supply chain, especially under the impact of emergencies such as the COVID-19, may lead to instability in the international food market and affect China's food imports (Stephens et al., 2020). In addition to the above factors, seed industry safety is also a very important factor. Seeds are the “chips" of agriculture, and the safety of the seed industry is directly related to food security. China needs to strengthen the self-reliance and self-reliance of seed industry technology, ensure independent and controllable seed sources, and ensure food security (Luo, 2023).

In the face of the food crisis, the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) has released the 2023 Global Food Crisis Report (GRFC), calling on the international community to work towards promoting the transformation of the global food crisis response mechanism and strengthening relevant international cooperation. The goal is to better prevent, predict and address the root causes of the food crisis, rather than responding to its impact only when it occurs. Therefore, it is very important to evaluate and judge the resilience level of the food system, strengthen it to cope with the risk impact brought by various uncertain and unstable factors, promote the transformation and upgrading of the system, and achieve food system security (Doherty et al., 2023).

In 2001, in order to adapt to the new changes in food production and distribution patterns, China divided 13 main food production areas, 7 main sales areas, and 11 basic balance areas, enabling advantageous food production areas to stably leverage their geographical resource advantages and ensuring that food production capacity gradually increased. There are 13 provinces in the main food producing areas, namely Heilongjiang, Henan, Shandong, Sichuan, Jiangsu, Hebei, Jilin, Anhui, Hunan, Hubei, Inner Mongolia, Jiangxi, and Liaoning. The main food producing areas have the core function of producing food products. This is its most direct and significant function of producing a large amount of food products by utilizing natural resources and consuming relevant production materials. These food products are not only the foundation for meeting the daily needs of domestic consumers, but also an important cornerstone for ensuring national food security and food safety. The main food sales areas include seven provinces, namely Beijing, Tianjin, Shanghai, Zhejiang, Fujian,

Guangdong, and Hainan. The main functions of these regions can be understood from both economic and social perspectives. At the economic level, the main food sales areas are usually relatively developed economically, but due to the large population and limited land, their food production often cannot meet their own needs, resulting in a significant demand gap. The food supply in these areas largely depends on the inflow of food from the main food producing areas to ensure the daily living needs of their residents. Therefore, one of the main functions of the main food sales areas is to serve as the consumption and distribution center of food, receiving and distributing food resources from the main food production areas. At the social level, as densely populated and economically active areas, the stability of food supply in the main food sales areas is of great significance for maintaining social stability and economic development. Therefore, these regions bear important responsibilities in terms of food security. By strengthening the construction of food circulation and reserve facilities, optimizing the food supply chain, the main food sales areas can ensure sufficient food supply in emergency situations to cope with potential food crises. Food production and marketing balance areas include Shanxi, Ningxia, Qinghai, Gansu, Xizang, Yunnan, Guizhou, Chongqing, Guangxi, Shaanxi, Xinjiang and other provinces. Although these regions have limited contributions to the national food production, they can basically maintain self-sufficiency and play a crucial role in ensuring national food security. For example, the production and sales balance zone plays the role of a “regulator" in the food market, balancing the supply and demand relationship of the food market by adjusting food production and sales volume, preventing excessive price fluctuations, and maintaining market stability

Based on the above background, this article summarizes the resilience of the food system into three dimensions: risk resistance, adaptability and adjustment, and innovation and change (Zuo & Ye, 2024; Hao & Tan, 2022). Risk resistance ability refers to the ability of the food system to effectively resist various risks and impacts and maintain stable system operation (Oliver et al., 2018). Adaptive adjustment ability refers to the ability of the food system to quickly make adaptive adjustments in the face of various external risks and impacts, enabling it to quickly recover to a stable operating state. The ability to innovate and transform refers to the ability of the food system to optimize and upgrade at a higher level by leveraging its organizational advantages when facing various risks and impacts. This article selects provincial-level panel data from 31 regions in China, and analyzes the resilience level, time and spatial distribution characteristics of China's main food production areas, main food sales areas, and production sales balance areas according to the division of different functional zones of food. At the same time, this article uses geographic detectors to select five main influencing factors to analyze the factor detection and interaction detection of different functional areas of food in China, and then proposes optimization strategies.

## 2.4 Research method

## 2.4.1 Entropy method

This article uses the entropy method to determine the weight of indicators and calculates the resilience values of the food system for 31 provinces (autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the central government) and different food functional zones in China from 2013 to 2022. Firstly, the range normalization method is used to obtain dimensionless data. Secondly, the information entropy is extracted, and then the indicator weights are determined.

## 2.4.2 Geodetector

This article selects a geographic detector model to further analyze the factors affecting the resilience of the food system. Geodetector is a statistical method that detects spatial differentiation and reveals its underlying driving factors. This model method has significant advantages in processing mixed type data due to its limited constraints (Song et al., 2020). The geographic detector includes four detectors: factor detection, interaction detection, risk detection, and ecological detection. This article comprehensively considers the needs of analyzing the resilience impact mechanisms of food systems in different functional areas of China and adopts factor detection and interaction detection as the analysis methods to measure the factors affecting the resilience of food systems.

## 3 Results and discussion

## 3.1 Evaluation of resilience level of food system in China's three major functional zones

## 3.1.1 Main food producing areas

At the overall level, this article uses the entropy method to evaluate the overall resilience level and resilience levels in different dimensions of China's main food producing areas. The results are shown in Fig. 1. From the data in the graph, it can be seen that the resilience level of China's main food producing areas has been increasing year by year. Although it decreased slightly in 2017, overall it has significantly improved. From 0.141 in 2013 to 0.657 in 2022, there has been an increase of 365.96%.

This is because: firstly, policy stability and sustained support. For many years, the Chinese government has attached great importance to food security and stabilized food production through a series of agricultural policies, including the implementation of food purchase protection price policies, agricultural subsidy policies, and the rural revitalization strategy proposed in recent years. The stable implementation of these policies provides a favorable development environment for the main production areas, stimulates the production enthusiasm of farmers, and thus enhances the resilience of food production. Secondly, the promotion of technological innovation. With the continuous innovation of agricultural technology, the efficiency and quality of food production have been significantly improved. The cultivation of new varieties, the improvement of agricultural mechanization level, and the application of intelligent and information technology in the agricultural field have all made food production more efficient and accurate, thereby enhancing the resilience of the main production areas. Thirdly, the improvement of infrastructure. In recent years, China has continuously strengthened the construction of agricultural infrastructure, including agricultural water conservancy, rural roads, storage facilities, etc. The improvement of these facilities has provided strong material support for food production. Especially in the face of sudden situations such as natural disasters, improved infrastructure can reduce the impact of disasters on food production and improve the recovery capacity of the main production areas.

In different dimensions, the main food producing areas have achieved significant improvements in risk resistance, adjustment and adaptability, and innovation and transformation capabilities. For example, in 2013, the risk resistance capacity of the main food producing areas was 0.075, and in 2022 it was 0.524, an increase of 598.67%. The adjustment adaptability of the main food producing areas was 0.171 in 2013 and 0.862 in 2022, an increase of 404.09%. The innovation and transformation capacity of the main food producing areas was 0.222 in 2013 and 0.646 in 2022, an increase of 190.99%. However, overall, the adaptability and innovation ability of China's main food producing areas are much higher than their risk resistance ability. There are two main factors contributing to the low-risk resistance of China's major food producing areas: firstly, frequent natural disasters. China is a country with frequent natural disasters, and disasters such as floods, droughts, and pests and diseases pose a serious threat to food production. Although disaster prevention and reduction capabilities have improved in recent years, the risk resistance of major food producing areas in the face of extreme weather events is still insufficient. Secondly, constraints on resources and environment. With population growth and economic development, food production is facing increasing resource and environmental pressures. The limited natural resources such as land and water sources limit the expansion space of food production, while environmental pollution and ecological damage also pose potential threats to food production.

## 3.1.3 Production and sales balance zone

The balance zone of food production and sales in China achieved a steady improvement from 2013 to 2022, as shown in Fig. 3. In 2013, it was 0.344, and in 2022 it was 0.660, an increase of 97.6%. The largest increase was 25.69% in 2019, and the largest decrease was -16.01% in 2016.

In terms of different sub dimensions, the greatest improvement was achieved in the dimension of innovation and change capability, at 214.54%. The smallest increase in risk resistance dimension was 39.97%. In addition, different from the main food production and marketing areas, the numerical results of China's food production and marketing balance area in three different dimensions are different. The most obvious time point is 2018. Before 2018, the resilience level of risk resistance is far higher than the ability to adjust, adapt and innovate, but after 2018, the ability to adjust, adapt and innovate has improved significantly, far higher than the risk resistance.

There have been significant changes in the numerical results of risk resistance, adjustment adaptability, and innovation and transformation ability in China's food production and sales balance zones around 2018, which are influenced by multiple factors. Firstly, before 2018, the resilience level of risk resistance was relatively high, which may be related to the policy orientation, market environment, and resource conditions at that time. The government's support policies for food production and sales balance zones may focus more on risk management and stable production to ensure the stability and security of food supply. In addition, the market environment at that time may have been relatively stable, with relatively small fluctuations in food prices, which also provided good risk resistance for the production and sales balance zone. Secondly, after 2018, the situation has changed. With the complex and ever-changing domestic and international market environment, the fluctuation of food prices has increased, and the market risks faced by the production and sales balance zone have also increased. In order to address this challenge, the production and sales balance zone has to strengthen its ability to adjust, adapt, and innovate. For example, adapting to changes in market demand by adjusting planting structure, optimizing resource allocation, and other means. Improve production efficiency and reduce production costs by introducing new technologies, promoting new varieties, and strengthening industry chain collaboration.

It is worth noting that the Chinese government has also increased its efforts in promoting agricultural transformation, upgrading, and modernization. A series of policy measures supporting agricultural innovation and promoting industrial upgrading have been successively introduced, providing a good development environment and policy guarantee for the production and sales balance zone, which further promotes the adjustment, adaptation, and innovation and transformation capabilities of the production and sales balance zone. For example, in 2018, the Chinese government issued the <Opinions of the Ministry of Agriculture on Vigorously Implementing the Rural Revitalization Strategy and Accelerating the Transformation and Upgrading of Agriculture>, which pointed out that we should promote the transformation of agriculture from total expansion to quality improvement as soon as possible, sing the main melody of promoting quality, green, and brand agriculture, and accelerate the transformation and upgrading of agriculture.

## 3.2 Analysis of the spatiotemporal pattern of resilience level of the food system in China's three major functional zones

## 3.2.1 Main food producing areas

In order to more clearly demonstrate the evolution characteristics of the resilience level of food systems in different functional areas of China, this article uses ArcGIS software to display the resilience level of food in 2013, 2016, 2019, and 2022. The spatiotemporal evolution of the main food producing areas is shown in Fig. 4.

In terms of the variation range, as shown in Fig. 4, the highest resilience level of China's main food producing areas is continuously improving, from 0.491085 to 0.583957 in 2013 to 0.519675–0.641647 in 2022. In terms of changing regions, the highest values in 2013 mainly included four provinces: Hebei, Henan, Shandong, and Jiangsu. However, in 2022, it will expand to five provinces: Heilongjiang, Hebei, Henan, Shandong, and Jiangsu.

Between 2013 and 2022, the resilience level of food in Heilongjiang Province has significantly improved. Analyzing the reasons for this is partly due to the unique natural conditions in Heilongjiang, which provide a solid foundation for food production. This place has fertile black soil, which is rich in organic matter and minerals, making it extremely suitable for crop growth. At the same time, Heilongjiang has abundant water resources and a suitable climate, providing stable water sources and climatic conditions for food production. These natural advantages make the food production in Heilongjiang province naturally resilient, able to maintain high yields and quality under various conditions. On the other hand, the reason is that the Heilongjiang government attaches great importance to agricultural development and has taken a series of measures to improve the resilience level of food. The government has increased investment in agricultural infrastructure, improved conditions such as farmland water conservancy facilities and road traffic and enhanced the disaster resistance capacity of food production. In addition, the government actively promotes agricultural technological innovation, promotes advanced planting techniques and agricultural machinery equipment, and improves the technological content and efficiency of food production. The implementation of these policy measures provides strong guarantees for the improvement of food resilience in Heilongjiang. For example, in 2022, the Heilongjiang Provincial Government established a three-level agricultural expert guidance group at the provincial, municipal, and county levels, forming a chain promotion service model of“experts agricultural technicians demonstration bases + demonstration subjects radiation driven households", accelerating the entry of advanced technologies into villages, households, and fields. Through WeChat, short videos, live streaming platforms and other means, we provide online technical guidance and training services to cultivate 20,000 high-quality farmers. At the same time, we will adhere to the combination of good varieties, good methods, good opportunities, and good fields, vigorously promote the application of modern intelligent agricultural machinery, strive to create a leading area for the promotion and application of large-scale, high-power, high-end intelligent agricultural machinery equipment, and achieve the transformation and upgrading of agricultural machinery equipment.

Through the analysis of the reasons for the improvement of food resilience in Xizang, we can see that first, the strict farmland protection system ensures the sustainability of food production. For example, since 2016, Xizang has adhered to the principle of storing food on the ground and storing food in technology, implemented the strictest farmland protection system, improved the quality of cultivated land, improved food production capacity, and ensured food security, effectively preventing the loss and destruction of cultivated land resources. At the same time, by strengthening the action to improve the quality of cultivated land, the fertility of cultivated land has been steadily improved, providing stable land resources for food production. Secondly, the increase in the scale of local food reserves also provides strong guarantees for the enhancement of food resilience. In recent years, Xizang has increased the scale and input of local food reserves to ensure that there are enough food reserves to ensure the people's food supply in response to emergencies and natural disasters. This increase in reserve capacity has enhanced the stability and reliability of food supply in Xizang. Thirdly, the popularization of high-quality seeds and the construction of high standard farmland provide a solid foundation for improving food production. In the past few years, Xizang has actively promoted fine varieties and has significantly improved the yield and quality of food crops through scientific planting methods and technologies. For example, data shows that in 2021, 1,981,900 mu of highland barley varieties will be planted in Xizang, with a coverage rate of 90.8%. At the same time, the promotion of high standard farmland construction projects has improved farmland infrastructure, enhanced the water and fertilizer retention capacity of farmland, and provided a good soil environment for food production. For example, the output of highland barley in Shigatze City accounts for about 50% of Xizang and is known as the “granary of Xizang". In recent years, the city has continued to promote the renovation of field facilities and the construction of high standard farmland. ## 3.3 Detection and analysis of resilience driving factors in the food system of China's three major functional zones

This article uses ArcGIS and Geodetector for factor detection and interaction detection to analyze the impact of various factors on the resilience of food systems in different functional areas of China in 2013 and 2022.

## 3.3.1 Main food producing areas

## (1) Factor detection analysis

From Table 3, it can be seen that among the factors affecting the resilience level of food in China's main food producing areas in 2013, the impact of unit area food yield is the highest. In 2022, the impact of X4 rural primary industry practitioners and X5 agricultural mechanization level on the resilience level of China's main food producing areas is the highest.

This is mainly because, firstly, as the main force of food production, the quantity and quality of rural primary industry practitioners directly affect the efficiency and stability of food production. When the number of employees is sufficient and the skill level is high, it can ensure the continuity and efficiency of food production, thereby enhancing the resilience of the main food producing areas. When facing external shocks, such as natural disasters and market price fluctuations, these practitioners can quickly adjust their production strategies, maintain stability in food production, and reduce risks. Secondly, the improvement of agricultural mechanization level is an important symbol of modernization of agricultural production and a key factor in enhancing the resilience of major food producing areas. With the popularization and deepening of agricultural mechanization, many heavy and repetitive tasks in the agricultural production process can be replaced by machinery, which not only improves production efficiency but also reduces production costs. Meanwhile, mechanized operations can reduce the impact of human factors on food production, improve the standardization and precision level of production, and further enhance the stability and reliability of food production. Thirdly, there is a mutually reinforcing relationship between rural primary industry practitioners and the level of agricultural mechanization. On the one hand, the increase and skill enhancement of practitioners provide strong support for the promotion and application of agricultural mechanization. On the other hand, the popularization of agricultural mechanization can further liberate the labor force, improve the labor efficiency of practitioners, and form a virtuous cycle. This mutually reinforcing relationship helps to enhance the overall resilience level of major food producing areas.

This is because, firstly, unit food area yield directly reflects agricultural production efficiency. In the food production and sales balance zone, the increase in yield per unit area means the effective utilization of agricultural production resources and the reduction of waste. This improvement in efficiency helps to increase the total food production, thereby enhancing the resistance of the production and sales balance zone to external market fluctuations and maintaining the stability of food supply and demand within the region. Secondly, the stability of yield per unit food area is a key factor in ensuring food security. Due to various factors such as climate, soil, and pests and diseases, food production fluctuates greatly. The stability of the yield per unit food area means that even in the face of unfavorable conditions, agricultural production can maintain a certain level of output, which is crucial for maintaining the food supply in the production and sales balance zone. Thirdly, the increase in yield per unit of food area is usually accompanied by the progress of agricultural technology and the optimization of agricultural production methods. By introducing new varieties, improving planting techniques, and strengthening field management, the yield per unit area can be increased, while reducing production costs and enhancing the competitiveness of agricultural production. These measures help to improve the agricultural production level of the production and sales balance zone, thereby enhancing its resilience.

## 4 Conclusion

This article first uses the entropy method to measure the resilience level of the food system in China's main food producing areas, main sales areas, and production sales balance areas from 2013 to 2022. Then, ArcGIS is used to analyze the spatiotemporal evolution patterns of different food functional areas in China in 2013, 2016, 2019, and 2022. Finally, the factors affecting the resilience level of China's nonfood functional areas were analyzed using a geographic detector model.

The main conclusions are as follows: (1) The resilience level of China's main food producing areas has significantly improved, and the overall resilience level of China's main food sales areas and production sales balance areas has steadily improved. (2) In terms of subdivision dimension evaluation, China's main food sales areas and food production areas have similar development trends, and the numerical results of adjusting adaptability and innovation and change capabilities are higher than risk resistance capabilities. There is a time interval of 2018 in the production and marketing balance area. Before 2018, the resilience level of risk resistance was much higher than the ability to adjust, adapt and innovate, but after 2018, the ability to adjust, adapt and innovate significantly improved, higher than the ability to resist risks. (3) In terms of spatiotemporal evolution, the resilience level of the main food producing areas is gradually expanding from four provinces to five provinces. The area with the lowest level of resilience is also gradually decreasing. In the main food sales areas, the changes in different provinces are relatively stable, but the resilience level of Shanghai continues to decrease. In terms of production and marketing balance areas, other provinces have not changed much, and Xizang has significantly improved. (4) In terms of influencing factors, unit food yield, multiple cropping index, rural primary industry personnel, and agricultural mechanization level are the main influencing factors on the resilience level of the food system in different functional areas of China. The interaction relationship between each influencing factor is mainly two factor enhancement and non-linear enhancement, and there is no other relationship.
